QUALITATIVE ADJUSTMENTS
-----------------------
  Two weak quarters   →  all bands × 0.90 (tighten). Trim price unchanged.
  Two strong quarters →  all bands × 1.10 (expand).  Trim price unchanged.
  Both set            →  tighten takes precedence.


SIGNALS
-------
  Deep  →  CMP < Buy Low
  Buy   →  Buy Low ≤ CMP ≤ Buy High
  Hold  →  Buy High < CMP ≤ Mid High
  Trim  →  CMP > Mid High


TRANCHES
--------
  - 3 tranches per stock, quadratic skew toward lower prices
  - CMP above buy zone  →  limits across Buy Low → Buy High
  - CMP inside buy zone →  floor = Buy Low × 0.9, ceiling = CMP
  - CMP below Buy Low   →  floor = CMP, ceiling = Buy Low
  - Live price fetched at generation time; no tranche ever placed above CMP
  - Budget split equally across tranches
