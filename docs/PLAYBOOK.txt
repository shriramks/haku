QUALITATIVE ADJUSTMENTS
-----------------------
  Two weak quarters   →  all bands × 0.90 (tighten). Trim price unchanged.
  Two strong quarters →  all bands × 1.10 (expand).  Trim price unchanged.
  Both set            →  tighten takes precedence.


SIGNALS
-------
  Deep  →  CMP < Buy Low
  Buy   →  Buy Low ≤ CMP ≤ Buy High
  Hold  →  Buy High < CMP ≤ Mid High
  Trim  →  CMP > Mid High


TRANCHES
--------
  Count:    2–8 tranches; driven by deployable ÷ suggested-per-tranche (2% of FY budget,
            floored at 1%). Deeper tranches get larger allocations (back-weighted).

  Pricing zones (live CMP fetched at generation time):
    CMP above buy zone  →  floor = max(24wk-low, Buy Low),  ceiling = Buy High
    CMP inside buy zone →  floor = max(24wk-low, Buy Low),  ceiling = CMP
    CMP below Buy Low   →  single tranche at CMP (deep-value entry at market)
    24wk-low ≥ CMP      →  floor = Buy Low (price is at 6-month low; 24wk-low ignored)

  No tranche is ever placed above CMP.

  Deploy Capital:  optional FY-level field. If set, deployable per stock =
                   min(stock remaining budget, deploy capital). If unset,
                   deployable = stock remaining budget.
